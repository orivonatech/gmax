About Us

G-MAX LTD

Your trusted connectivity partner

G-MAX Ltd is an Internet Service Provider (ISP) company registered in Rwanda with a license issued by Rwanda Utility Regulatory Agency (RURA) and in partnership with Korea Telecom Rwanda Networks (KTRN) to provide Internet access to end users.

We believe in looking towards the future with great visions and endless inventions in developing technologies, products and services to suit our customers’ needs both today and in the future to live up to the company’s motto — “Always Ahead”.
