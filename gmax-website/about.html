About Us

G-MAX LTD

An Internet Service Provider (ISP) company registered in Rwanda with a license issued by Rwanda Utility Regulatory Agency (RURA) and in partnership with Korea Telecom Rwanda Networks (KTRN) to provide Internet access to end users.

We believe in looking towards the future with great visions and endless inventions in developing technologies, products and services to suit our customers’ needs both today and in the future to live up to the company’s motto – “Always Ahead”.

By constantly searching for technological innovations that will provide performance-enhancing solutions for our customers, and striving for the highest standards of service, we will continue to champion the advancement of Internet and Business Solutions Service Provider in Rwanda especially in services for corporate customers.
