ABOUT G-MAX

Always Ahead in technology and connectivity

G-MAX Ltd is a Rwandan Internet Service Provider licensed by RURA. In partnership with Korea Telecom Rwanda Networks (KTRN), we provide quality internet and ICT solutions for homes, businesses and institutions.

Our goal is to stay ahead of technology by continuously developing products and services that meet the changing needs of our customers.
