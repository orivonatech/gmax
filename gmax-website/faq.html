Frequently Asked Questions

What is G-MAX Ltd?+

G-MAX Ltd is an Internet Service Provider (ISP) company registered in Rwanda. It is licensed by the Rwanda Utility Regulatory Agency (RURA) and partners with Korea Telecom Rwanda Networks (KTRN) to provide internet access to end users.

Where is G-MAX Ltd located?+

G-MAX Ltd is located at KG 5Av. 2nd floor UBUMWE HOUSE, Kimihurura Sector, Gasabo District, Kigali City, Rwanda. The P.O. Box is 2838, Kigali-Rwanda.

Does G-MAX Ltd provide services to businesses?+

Yes, G-MAX Ltd provides internet and IT solutions for corporate customers.

What principles guide G-MAX Ltd's services for corporate customers?+

G-MAX Ltd's services for corporate customers are guided by three principles:

  • Stability: Providing quality and stable network systems with high system security.
  • Flexibility: Developing services and solutions that are flexible, customizable to business needs, and able to answer corporate customers' needs.
  • Dedication: Providing quality after-sale services, consultations, and quick problem-solving through a 24/7 team of experts.
